How the Loan Renewal Demo Works

A governed, human-in-the-loop workflow: an AI agent prepares a credit memo, and a senior officer makes the final call — every step audited.

GOVERNANCE Human-in-the-loop

  • AI prepares the work; a human makes the call.
  • Large (≥$5M) or KYC-pending cases always escalate to an approver.
  • No AI decision is final on its own.

ACCURACY No invented numbers

  • Every figure comes from a governed bank-system call.
  • Agent is only allowed to cite real data.
  • Memos are validated before submission.

AUDITABILITY Full trace

  • Who did what, when, and why — recorded.
  • Approval decision logged under the approver's identity.
  • Official memo archived per case/year.

EFFICIENCY Faster turnaround

  • Agent fetches + drafts in minutes, not days.
  • Approver reviews a ready memo, not raw data.
  • Client is notified the moment a decision lands.
