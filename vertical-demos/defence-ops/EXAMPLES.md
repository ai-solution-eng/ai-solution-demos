# Example tactical questions

## Smart Context

- What are the immediate tactical implications of what the feeds show?
- How should the actions be prioritised?
- Give me the SITREP on the feeds and the alerts.
- Is there any coordinated movement across the sectors?

## Alerts and imagery together

Select two or three feeds, press **Generate Alerts**, then ask:

- Which alerts can you confirm from the imagery, and which can you not?
- Which alert needs action first, and why?
- Is anything in the feeds missing from the alerts?

## Single feed

- How many military vehicles can you see?
- Make a best estimate of the strength of the observed forces.
- What is the likely intent of the observed forces?
- What would you task the next ISR collection to confirm?
